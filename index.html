To My Future Wife ♡

Hey en pondatti,

Innum 5 years kalichu indha letter ah nee padikkumbodhu namma enga iruppom nu enakku theriyadhu. Aana innikum madhiriye appovum nee en kaiya pidichuttu irukkanum nu aasai paduren.

Namma own ah oru chinna veedu, rendu perukum separate dreams, busy schedules, chinna chinna sandaigal, random laughs, midnight conversations nu namakkana oru azhagana life ah imagine panren.

Nee eppovume dream panna andha doctor life ah achieve pannanum. Un success ah pakkathula irundhu celebrate pannanum. Naanum ennoda career la grow aaganum. Namma rendu perum individual ah achieve panni, onna serndhu oru life build pannanum.

Kalai, naan perfect person nu solla maaten. Neraya mistakes pannirukken, innum neraya things kathukanum. Aana unakku better partner ah irukka daily try pannanum nu aasai paduren.

Life la evlo difficulties vandhaalum, namma rendu perum yen indha journey ah start pannom nu marandhura koodadhu.

Oru naal namma rendu perum pakkathula ukkandhu namma past ah thirumbi paakkumbodhu, “Namma evlo kashtapattu, evlo grow aagi, finally namma dream life ah build pannitom la” nu sollanum.

Un dreams ah nee achieve pannanum. En dreams ah naan achieve pannanum. Aana namma rendu peroda biggest dream namma serndhu oru azhagana life vaazhuradha irukkanum.

Future la irukkura en Kalai-ku oru request... indha letter padikkumbodhum ennoda kaiya vidaama irukkanum.

I love you di. Innikum, appovum, eppovum. ♡
Forever yours.
